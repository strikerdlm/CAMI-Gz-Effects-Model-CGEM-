## Rationale (per IJNMBE updated Aims and Scope, cited verbatim in the rejection email)

> International Journal for Numerical Methods in Biomedical Engineering is no longer accepting submissions based purely on machine and deep learning methods applied to biomedical problems.
>
> However, using these methods to solve differential equations or to accelerate numerical methods is within the scope.

The IJNMBE Aims and Scope text also distinguishes Mathematical model (Fundamentals), Computational method (Fundamentals), and Applications (3D required except in special cases). The rejection email categorises the present manuscript as outside all three: a purely ML application to a biomedical problem.

The scope change is categorical; appeal is not viable. CGEM moved to *Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine* (CMPB, Elsevier, Q1) per `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-05-17-cgem-cmpb-pivot-design.md`.
